# CalPal Code of Conduct

## Our Community Values

CalPal is committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative open-source community. Contributors may have different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives, and these differences should be treated as a benefit to the project.

Our goal is to create an environment where people feel comfortable asking questions, suggesting ideas, reporting problems, and contributing to the project.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Be patient and helpful toward new contributors.
- Respect different ideas, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Accept constructive criticism professionally.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the project rather than individuals.
- Help maintain a welcoming and productive environment.
- Respect the privacy of other community members.

Questions and mistakes are a normal part of contributing to an open-source project. Contributors should help each other learn rather than discourage participation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable within the CalPal community:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or intentionally offensive comments.
- Threatening or hostile behavior.
- Repeatedly disrupting project discussions.
- Posting another person's private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
- Deliberately creating an environment that discourages others from participating.
- Continuing unacceptable behavior after being asked to stop.

Technical disagreements are acceptable, but they should remain respectful and focused on improving the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in the CalPal project, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull requests
- Code reviews
- Project discussions
- Other communication made on behalf of CalPal

The standards apply to both contributors and project maintainers.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report the incident privately to a CalPal project maintainer.

Reports should include relevant information about what occurred, such as where the incident happened and any comments or messages involved.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as possible. A person reporting a violation should not be required to confront the person whose behavior they reported.

If a report involves a particular maintainer, it should be directed to another CalPal maintainer whenever possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports and enforcing these community standards.

Depending on the nature and severity of a violation, maintainers may take actions including:

1. Providing a private or public warning.
2. Removing inappropriate comments or other content.
3. Temporarily restricting participation in the project.
4. Permanently removing a participant from the project in serious or repeated cases.

Maintainers should consider the context and severity of an incident before taking action. Reports should be reviewed fairly, and enforcement should focus on protecting a respectful and productive community.

Project maintainers are also expected to follow this Code of Conduct and should not receive special treatment when a complaint involves their own behavior.

## Our Commitment

The success of an open-source project depends on people being willing to participate and work together. CalPal welcomes contributions from people with different levels of technical experience and encourages community members to help one another.

By participating in the CalPal project, community members agree to follow these standards and contribute to an environment that is respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
